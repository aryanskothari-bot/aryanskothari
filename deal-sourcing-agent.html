FAQ

Good to know.

Where does the data come from?

Company and research agents search the live web on each run and list every source used. Stock prices come from free market-data feeds (Yahoo Finance, with Stooq as backup), up to two years of daily closes.

How is the six-month outlook built?

Two views are combined. The quant desk resamples the stock's own daily returns over the last year to simulate 3,000 possible six-month paths, which gives a range and the odds of being up or down. The research desk writes bull, base and bear cases with probabilities from fundamentals and upcoming catalysts. The desk view weighs both.

Is this Jane Street's model?

No. Trading firms keep their models private. The trading-desk view uses widely published principles those desks are known for: think in expected value, size risk by volatility, compare what the market implies with what has happened, and always ask what would make you wrong.

Can I rely on it?

Use it as a first screen. Figures are AI estimates from public sources and simulations from past prices; neither predicts the future. Nothing here is investment advice.
