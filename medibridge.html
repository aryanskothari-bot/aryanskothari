FAQ

Questions patients ask.

Is it safe to have surgery abroad?

It can be, at internationally accredited hospitals (for example JCI) with experienced surgeons. Check accreditation, the surgeon's record and the complication policy, and plan aftercare at home before you travel.

How accurate are the prices?

They are estimates built from live web prices and typical costs. Use them to compare options, then ask the hospital for a written, itemised quote.

How long should I stay after surgery?

Each package shows a typical stay. Your surgeon decides when it is safe to fly; longer stays are usual after joint, spine and heart procedures.

Do I need a medical visa?

Some countries offer a medical visa or e-visa. Each package lists a visa note; always check the embassy website for your nationality.

Does MediBridge book or take payment?

No. MediBridge helps you compare and prepares your enquiry. You deal directly with the hospital.
