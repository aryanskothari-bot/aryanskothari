1. Disclaimers: please read

Not financial, investment, legal or tax advice

Diligence Desk produces an automated first screen from public web sources. It is not an audit, a valuation, a fairness opinion, a legal review or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any company or security. Scores, verdicts, price ranges and six-month scenarios are estimates and simulations, not forecasts or promises. Past prices do not predict future returns. Always do your own diligence and take advice from qualified professionals before any transaction.

Not medical advice

MediBridge is a planning and comparison tool. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment or assess whether you are fit to travel. Prices, complication rates, recovery times and "true cost" figures are estimates. Only licensed clinicians can decide on your care: speak to your own doctor before booking treatment abroad. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

AI can be wrong

Both tools use AI models that can misread sources, miss information or state errors with confidence. Every output shows its sources where available; check them. A claim marked "unverifiable" is not false, it means no public evidence was found either way.
